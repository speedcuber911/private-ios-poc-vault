  • India new tax regime for FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27.

  • Health and education cess: 4% of income tax.
  • CTC here means taxable gross salary before income tax. Employer PF, gratuity, insurance, reimbursements, ESOPs, surcharge, old-regime deductions, and professional tax are not included unless you enter them as monthly deductions.
