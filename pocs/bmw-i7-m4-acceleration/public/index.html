Model notes

The first 0-200 km/h section uses published acceleration tables. The 50s and 100s readings require a limiter assumption.

The 0-200 km/h points are linearly interpolated, then distance is calculated with trapezoidal integration. For the long-run model, the i7 ramps from 200 to 240 km/h by 25s. The Driver's Package M4 passes through 250 km/h at 24s and reaches 290 km/h by 45s; the standard-limiter case holds at 250 km/h from 24s onward.

Those post-200 km/h ramp times are modeling assumptions, so the exact 50s/100s distance can move with road, tires, surface, weather, battery state, fuel load, and limiter package. The direction is stable: the M4 leads early and keeps widening the gap if it has the higher top-speed package.
